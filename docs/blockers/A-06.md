## SUPERSEDED 2026-09-07 — no longer blocked

Appended rather than rewritten, per the append-only rule on `docs/blockers/**`.

Zaeem asked why the uploads could not be automated. Investigating that produced a better path
than the manual one this document assumed:

* **Computer use cannot drive a browser.** Browsers are granted at tier "read" — visible in
  screenshots, clicks and typing blocked. Claude-in-Chrome is the tool designed for it, but
  `list_connected_browsers` returned `[]`, so no browser was connected.
* **The API can do almost all of it.** `videos.insert` uploads fine; it is only *publishing*
  that an unverified app cannot do. So `scripts/seed_uploads.py` uploads all five privately
  with their caption tracks attached, and the manual step shrinks from ~25 minutes of uploading,
  metadata entry and subtitle attachment to about two minutes of visibility toggles.

Outcome: all five videos uploaded (2008 quota units), all three in-scope proofs **PASS**, and
ADR-000 gap **G-2 closed** — `tfmt="srt"` accepted live on the first attempt. See
`docs/proofs/A-06.md`.
